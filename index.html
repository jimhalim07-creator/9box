How the grid reads your appraisal scores

Enter each colleague's appraisal ratings on the usual 1 to 5 scale, one for performance and one for commitment. On that scale 3.5 means fully meets requirements. The grid turns each score into Low, Medium or High and places the person for you.

Performance looks back at how well someone delivered against their goals and role expectations this cycle. Commitment looks at alignment: how strongly someone lives CARE's mission and values, and how reliable and trustworthy they are, whatever their output this cycle.

Open any box title to see what it means and what a manager and HR should do about it. The four tiers group the nine boxes for quick scanning.

Treat placements as the start of a conversation, not a verdict. Calibrate as a team using concrete evidence, revisit every cycle, and watch for bias before letting one placement drive a pay, promotion or exit decision on its own. A high performer with low commitment deserves the same scrutiny as a low performer, because results should never excuse a trust or values problem.
